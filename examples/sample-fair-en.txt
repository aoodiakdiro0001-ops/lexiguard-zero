MUTUAL SERVICES AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (balanced text, low-signal demo)

1. SCOPE AND MILESTONES
Provider shall deliver the services in Schedule 1 in three milestones,
each with written acceptance criteria.

2. PAYMENT
Fees are split 30% on signing, 40% on mid-delivery and 30% on acceptance.
Invoices are due within Net 15 days.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Each party keeps its pre-existing intellectual property. Upon receipt of
full payment, Provider assigns the final deliverables' intellectual
property rights to Client, excluding Provider's pre-existing tools and
libraries.

4. REVISIONS
Provider includes three rounds of revisions per deliverable; further
revisions are billed at the hourly rate and scoped in writing.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 15 days' written notice; upon
termination Client pays for all work performed to that date.

6. LIABILITY
Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months
preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect damages.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Obligations last three years from disclosure and exclude information
already public or independently developed.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of the country where Provider is
established, with courts of that country having jurisdiction.
